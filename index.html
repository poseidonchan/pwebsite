Research focus

AI for biology. Efficient intelligence.

I develop generative models for biological discovery and design, and low-precision methods that make large AI models more efficient to train and deploy.

I am a PhD student at the University of Maryland, advised by Heng Huang and working closely with Wei Chen. Previously, I received my B.S. in Biological Science from Tsinghua University.

Research interests

AI for biology
Generative modeling for virtual cells, mRNA design, and protein design. I develop models to predict cellular responses and generate biological sequences with desired functions.
Efficient machine learning
Post-training quantization, quantization-aware training, and efficient inference for language and multimodal models. I work on low-precision methods that reduce memory and compute costs while preserving model capabilities.
Long-term vision

My long-term mission is to build a future in which biological systems can be understood and responsibly programmed to improve human life, while intelligence is accessible to everyone. I pursue this vision through two complementary directions: AI for biology, to move from understanding life to designing it responsibly; and efficient machine learning, to pair stronger intelligence with substantially lower inference costs so advanced AI becomes a resource everyone can use.
